KDOAS Alert Flyer Links

These are the web addresses printed on the Kentucky Drug Overdose Alert System (KDOAS) public alert flyer.

If you see someone who may be overdosing

  • Overdose prevention supply locations and hours

    https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dph/Pages/hrsp.aspx

  • Find naloxone near you or order it by mail. Training videos and graphics are available on the site.

    https://findnaloxone.ky.gov/Pages/index.aspx

If you or someone you know needs help

  • Never Use Alone hotline: overdose prevention, detection, life-saving crisis response and medical intervention services.

    https://neverusealone.com/

  • Search real-time openings for substance use disorder treatment, mental health treatment, and recovery housing.

    https://findhelpnow.org/ky
